# Business Brief — Purposeology™ / PurposeQuest International

## Who We Are
DrG Alfred Palmer is the founder and Senior Purposeologist of Purposeology™, President/CEO of DrG Alfred Palmer International and PurposeQuest International, and CEO of The Foundation for Contemporary Life Health Services (Washington, D.C.). Author of 20+ books including the international bestseller *Purpose-ology: The Science of Purpose*. DrG traces the founding of Purposeology™ to a 2010 encounter with mentor Dr. Myles Munroe, and has shared platforms with Les Brown, Willie Jolley, George Fraser, and Myles Munroe.

## What Purposeology™ Is
Purposeology™ is a proprietary purpose discovery and deployment framework that helps people find why they were born and what they are meant to be doing with their lives. It moves people through five stages: **find, focus, form, follow, fruition.**

Core distinctions used externally:
- **Chronos vs. Kairos** — sequential time vs. appointed/divine timing.
- **Purpose Fingerprint™** — the unique convergence of Kairos, Geography, and Identity that makes each person's calling unrepeatable.

The underlying 26-concept framework and Concept Dictionary are proprietary and internal-only — never enumerated, defined, or reconstructed in outward-facing content.
