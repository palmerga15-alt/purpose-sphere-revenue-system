# When the Nameplate Comes Down

## Core message

A title may describe your assignment, but it does not define your identity. Purpose remains when the office changes, the position ends, or the nameplate comes down.

## Short-form script

One day, the nameplate comes down.

The office belongs to someone else.  
The title moves to another name.  
The applause grows quiet.

Then comes the deeper question:

**Who are you when the position can no longer introduce you?**

Your title was never your purpose. It was an assignment—one place, for one season, where your purpose could make a contribution.

Purpose is deeper than a role. It is the original intent that continues to speak through every season of your life.

So lead well. Serve fully. Contribute generously.

But never allow a temporary position to become your permanent identity.

When the nameplate comes down, purpose remains.

## Closing line

Rediscover the you that always was.

## Suggested caption

Positions change. Titles end. Purpose remains. If your identity has become tied to what you do, it may be time to rediscover who you are—and why you are here.

## Visual direction

Open on a close-up of a nameplate being removed from an office door. Transition to the person leaving the building, then walking forward into natural light. End with the Purposeology® mark and the words: **Purpose remains.**
