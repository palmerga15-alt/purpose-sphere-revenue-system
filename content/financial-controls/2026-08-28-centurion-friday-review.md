## 3. Expense controls

Review every new or recurring expense.

- Keep: directly supports delivery, sales, or required operations.
- Reduce: useful, but cost exceeds its current contribution.
- Pause: not connected to the current revenue asset.
- Cancel: duplicate, unused, or no longer aligned.
