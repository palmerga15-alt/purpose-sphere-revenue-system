## Messaging Pillars (use consistently across all speaking-stream content)

1. **Chronos vs. Kairos** — the speaking-stream's signature reframe: leadership/purpose content that distinguishes calendar time from appointed/divine timing. Works as a keynote hook, a deck section header, and a one-sheet tagline element.
2. **The Purpose Fingerprint™** — the individual-level payoff audience members walk away with: their calling is unrepeatable, so generic leadership advice isn't enough.
3. **The five-stage journey (find, focus, form, follow, fruition)** — the structural backbone of the keynote itself; gives bookers a legible arc rather than a loose motivational talk, and gives DrG a repeatable keynote architecture across circuits.
4. **Proof over polish** — directly answers the research's booker-priority finding (full-length video and verified references beat sizzle reels and curated quotes). This pillar should shape *how* the EPK talks about itself, not just what it claims.
5. **A lineage carried forward, not a title claimed** — DrG's connection to Dr. Myles Munroe is framed as personal mentorship (the 2010 encounter) and continuing a tradition, never as replacing Munroe or claiming his mantle. Paired consistently with the Titans of Public Speaking honor as the *contemporary*, independently verifiable peer credential — so the Munroe reference is about origin story, and the Titans honor is about current standing.

## Direct-Outreach Angle: George Fraser's Power Networking Conference

This is the offer spec's single most actionable near-term target (~4 months out, inside the established-tier lead-time window, built on a genuine existing peer relationship). This needs its own one-pager, distinct from the general-public EPK — written *to* Fraser's team, not *at* a general booker.

- Opens by naming the shared honor directly and specifically — "jointly honored as Titans of Public Speaking alongside you, Les Brown, and Willie Jolley at Operation HOPE's Hope Global Forum" — this is the one piece of proof that makes this outreach different from a cold pitch. Lead with it in the first line, not buried in a bio paragraph.

Customization-to-audience is explicitly what the research says bookers read as a professionalism signal — this pitch should demonstrate it, not just claim it.
- Positions the ask around relationship and mutual audience value first, consistent with the offer spec's note that this may be pursued as a strategic/relationship-tier booking rather than a standard-fee pitch — **do not state a number in this one-pager**; let the relationship and the in-person conversation carry the fee discussion.

**Format:** a one-page PDF or short direct email, addressed to Fraser's team specifically — not a version of the general EPK. Should be produced as its own short deliverable once this angle is approved.

- **Veteran-services circuit:** Strong fit via DrG's U.S. Air Force background and the existing "Operation: Rediscover Purpose" program name (already established per the brief — reuse rather than inventing a new veteran-specific program title). The Warrior Symposium (Aug 5–6, DMV) is a relationship-building opportunity per the offer spec, not a 2026 paid-booking target — content here should support in-person conversation, not a formal pitch deck.
- **Corporate/organizational buyers:** Best fit for the AI-thesis-extension topic ("From Information to Formation") since it bridges directly to the active webinar motion and gives L&D buyers a reason to see this as connected to Purposeology's broader offering, not a standalone speaking ask.
- **Black-leadership/HBCU circuit:** **Flagged as pending user sign-off**, per the offer spec's explicit note that this segment is a new addition not yet confirmed as a formal audience segment. The angles above (the "Purpose, Power, and the Room You're In" topic, and the Fraser outreach one-pager) are written so they can proceed on relationship grounds (the Fraser outreach is time-sensitive) without requiring the segment to be formally adopted — but broader public marketing toward this circuit (bureau listings framed around it, HBCU-specific one-sheet language) should wait for that sign-off.
- **Psychology-adjacent institutions:** Per the offer spec, this brief-listed segment is not a natural fit for the speaking offer and has been excluded here, consistent with the offer spec's own scoping.
