# Romulus Weekly Market-Development Priorities

## Weekly objective

Convert Purposeology® visibility into qualified conversations and near-term revenue while keeping the paid Purpose Discovery webinar as the primary offer.

## Top five priorities

### 1. Put the Purpose Discovery webinar on sale

Finalize the title, promise, date, price within the approved $47–$97 range, registration path, and payment link. The offer must be clear enough for a prospect to understand and purchase without explanation.

### 2. Begin DMV chamber outreach

Send tailored outreach to:

- Greater Washington Board of Trade
- DC Chamber of Commerce
- Prince George's Chamber of Commerce
- Montgomery County Chamber of Commerce

Position **Why Before AI** as an executive briefing or organizational workshop. Lead with relevance to leadership, workforce readiness, responsible adoption, and human impact.

### 3. Create the webinar promotion sequence

Prepare one core message and adapt it for:

- Email
- LinkedIn
- Facebook
- Instagram
- Direct outreach to warm contacts

Each message should direct people to one registration action.

### 4. Advance speaking opportunities

Review open calls and prioritize only those with strong alignment, credible access, meaningful audience reach, or realistic revenue potential. Track submission deadline, decision date, contact, and required follow-up.

### 5. Build the qualified-conversation pipeline

Identify 10 organizations or leaders whose work intersects with leadership, workforce development, behavioral health, AI readiness, or purpose-centered transformation. Secure at least three conversations.

## Weekly scorecard

| Measure | Target |
|---|---:|
| Webinar registration page completed | 1 |
| Webinar invitations sent | 25 |
| Chamber outreach emails sent | 4 |
| Qualified prospects added | 10 |
| Executive conversations scheduled | 3 |
| Speaking opportunities advanced | 3 |
| Paid registrations | 5 minimum |

## Decisions required

- Final webinar title and promise
- Webinar date and delivery platform
- Final price between $47 and $97
- Primary audience for the first session
- One lead offer for chamber outreach
- Follow-up owner and timing for every outreach message

## What can wait

- New offer development
- Additional course levels
- Nonessential website redesign
- Broad social posting without a clear call to action
- New tools or subscriptions that do not support sales or delivery

## Three concrete next actions

1. Publish the webinar registration and checkout path.
2. Send the four chamber outreach messages.
3. Contact the first 10 qualified prospects and request a conversation.

## End-of-week definition of success

The webinar is purchasable, outreach is documented, at least three qualified conversations are scheduled, and the next revenue action is unmistakably clear.
