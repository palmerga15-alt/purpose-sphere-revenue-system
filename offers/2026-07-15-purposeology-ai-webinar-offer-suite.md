# Offer Spec: "Purposeology & AI" Webinar Launch Suite

**Date prepared:** 2026-07-14 | **Event date:** 2026-07-15 (Wednesday, T-1 day at time of writing)
**Pipeline stage:** offer-architect (stage 2 of market-signal-researcher → offer-architect → content-angle-strategist)
**Grounded in:** `business-brief.md` (root) and `/research/2026-07-purposeology-and-ai-webinar.md`

This suite covers two offers that work together as a single funnel:

1. **Offer A — The Webinar** (top-of-funnel, lead capture)
2. **Offer B — The Immediate Next-Step Offer** (the "most urgent, most immediate revenue path" — presented at/after the webinar), itself in two parts: a low-ticket fast-action book offer and the core paid workshop.

A third, non-monetized element (certification-school waitlist capture) is included because the brief lists it as priority #2 in the pipeline and the webinar is a natural place to start the list — but per the Purpose Council's directive to prove delivery infrastructure before opening new build fronts, it is explicitly **not** sold as a paid offer today.

## Offer A: "Purposeology & AI" Webinar

**What it is:** Live webinar, ~60–75 minutes (45–50 min presentation + 15–20 min live Q&A), delivered via the existing HeyGen avatar presentation (native TTS voice) over the existing PowerPoint deck (poster-art background). Free registration, single live session with a replay window offered to registrants.

**Format/delivery rationale:** The market-signal report's webinar-funnel benchmarks (live CTA conversion up to 47%, replay attendance ~29% average, replays now driving 2.4x unique viewers vs. live, 58% of webinar-sourced opportunities first-touching the replay) are standard-model benchmarks for **free, registration-gated** webinars — the report does not contain a paid-webinar-entry benchmark for this category. Free entry is therefore the model grounded in the report's own numbers; a paid-entry version would be an invented price point. **Recommendation: free registration**, monetization deferred to Offer B.

**Day/time:** July 15, 2026 is a Wednesday, which the report identifies as benchmarking as the best-performing webinar day; if time-of-day is still flexible, 11 AM PT / 2 PM ET is cited as the highest-attendance window. (Scheduling note, not a pricing decision — flagging per the report's own "act before July 15" callout.)

**Who it's for (brief audience segments — no new segments introduced):**
- Individuals in career/faith/midlife/post-military transition — primary draw, given the report's finding that AI anxiety discourse has shifted from job-loss to purpose-loss framing.
- Faith-adjacent / spiritual-but-not-religious audience — the report's strongest, most current anxiety data (54% of practicing Christians would trust AI to help find meaning; 57% see AI as a threat) sits here.
- Psychology-adjacent institutions (clinics, universities, corporate wellness) — relevant given the report's clinical citation linking meaning deficit to depression risk; treat as a secondary/partnership audience for this specific event, not the primary registrant.
- Veterans — included, but per the report's finding that veteran sentiment skews optimistic (not anxious) about AI, this segment should be positioned toward the webinar as "translate your edge" rather than anxiety relief. This is a note for the content-angle-strategist, not a new segment.
- Corporate/L&D buyers — present as observers/scouts for the speaking and corporate-training streams (priorities #3–4) rather than primary webinar registrants; the report shows this buyer is currently sold skills/productivity almost exclusively, leaving a meaning/retention angle open, but that sale happens downstream of this webinar, not at it.

**Price:** Free registration. (Grounded above; not a placeholder — the report supports this as the standard model.)

**Deliverables/outcomes (framed by the five-stage journey, no internal framework exposed):**
- Attendees leave with a reframe: AI as an information layer, Purposeology™ as the direction-and-meaning layer that determines what to do with that information.
- A first, guided taste of the **find** stage — a prompt to name what's pulling at them right now, delivered live.
- A clear, named next step into **focus** — which is Offer B below.

**Path to the priority above it:** This is the top of funnel for priority #1 (books/seminars/workshops). Registrants who do not convert on Offer B flow into the email sequence (asset already built) for later workshop cohorts; convertors become the qualified pool that later feeds the certification-school waitlist (priority #2) and, for standout or well-positioned attendees (executives, L&D contacts, veteran-service-org contacts), the speaking/corporate pipeline (priorities #3–4).

**Dependencies/assets:**
- *Already built per the brief:* VSL scripts ("The New Reveal" framework), social rollout, email sequences, PowerPoint deck, HeyGen avatar presentation.
- *Still needed (flagging, not building):* a registration/landing page and confirmation-email tech if not already live.
- *Confirmed live:* replay hosting is set up and ready, which matters given the report shows replay traffic materially outperforms live-only capture (58% of opportunities first-touch the replay).

## Offer B: The Immediate Next-Step Offer (books/seminars/workshops priority)

This is the "most urgent, most immediate revenue path" offer per the brief's priority #1. It is presented live at the webinar's close and reinforced in the post-webinar email sequence. It has two tiers so the funnel captures revenue at two friction levels: a low-ticket fast-action item, and the core paid workshop.

### B1 — Fast-Action Offer (presented live, time-boxed)

**What it is:** DrG's existing bestselling book, *Purpose-ology: The Science of Purpose*, offered as a decisive-action bonus to attendees who take the next step before a stated deadline.

**Deadline: July 20, 2026, 11:59 PM ET** (5 days after the webinar). Set to give replay viewers — 58% of registrants first-touch the replay per the research report — a real window to claim it, while still closing 2 days ahead of the July 22 workshop so the funnel sequence (fast-action bonus → workshop enrollment) doesn't overlap.

**Why this offer:** It uses an asset that already exists (the book is already published) rather than requiring new content — directly aligned with the Purpose Council's instruction to strengthen proof/delivery on existing assets before opening new build fronts. It is also the literal top item in priority #1 ("books, seminars, and workshops").

**Price:** **Placeholder — not in the report.** The market-signal report contains no benchmark for this book's retail or webinar-bundle price (its one book-pricing data point, *Ikigai 2.0*, is explicitly flagged unverified/blocked in the report and should not be used as a stand-in). Use the book's existing retail list price if one is already set; if a bundled bonus (e.g., a short companion worksheet tied to the "find" stage) is added, that bundle premium is also unvalidated and should be confirmed before the webinar, not invented here.

**Deliverables:** The book itself, framed as a "find"-stage tool — the reader's on-ramp to seeing their situation through the Purposeology lens, no internal concepts named or listed.

**Dependencies:** Existing published book (no build needed) + fulfillment logistics (digital delivery vs. physical shipping) — confirm which is already in place; this is an operational gap to flag, not a content gap.

### B2 — Core Offer: Workshop (the primary immediate-revenue vehicle)

**What it is:** A live, structured workshop — a half-day (3–4 hour) virtual intensive, delivered by DrG (distinct from the webinar's HeyGen-avatar delivery — the workshop is the higher-trust, higher-price tier and should carry DrG's own presence to justify that step-up). Small enough group size to allow structured interaction, not a mass broadcast.

**Date:** **July 22, 2026 (Wednesday)** — one week after the webinar, close enough to hold momentum from the webinar and the B1 fast-action book offer while leaving a one-week window to finalize workshop logistics (see Dependencies below).

**Who it's for:** Webinar attendees who self-select as ready to move past awareness into structured work — primarily the individuals-in-transition and faith-adjacent segments per the brief; veterans framed per the report's "translate your edge" angle rather than anxiety relief.

**Price/packaging:** **Set by decision, not derived from a report benchmark** — the report contains no direct comp for a single workshop/seminar ticket (its closest reference points are a different product category: life-purpose coach certifications $3,000–$7,500 for 3–9 month programs, an AI-coaching certification at $1,495/yr, keynote fees $2,500–$75,000+ — none used to back into this number).
  - **General admission (live virtual, group Q&A): $497.**
  - **VIP tier (general admission + small-group breakout or written follow-up on their "find" answer from the webinar): $997.**
  - Payment plan (e.g., split into 2 installments) recommended as a standard conversion lever, especially at the VIP tier.

**Deliverables/outcomes (five-stage framing, no internal framework exposed):**
- Structured movement from **find** into **focus** — participants leave with a written, personal articulation of the direction they're choosing to pursue, not a completed plan.
- A concrete bridge into **form** — named as the next stage, positioned as what a deeper engagement (coaching, a future workshop tier, or eventually the certification school) would build out.
- A clear, explicit invitation into whichever of the next-priority streams fits the attendee: certification-school waitlist (priority #2, list-building only — no paid seat sold yet), a scheduled call for corporate/organizational buyers (priorities #3–4), or nothing further for individuals who are satisfied at this stage.

**Path to the priority above it:** Workshop completers who are corporate/organizational contacts or who stand out as high-affinity individuals are the qualified pool for the speaking and Fortune 500 corporate-training streams (priorities #3–4). Workshop completers broadly (not just standouts) are the natural first cohort for the certification-school waitlist (priority #2) — but again, waitlist only, not a paid seat, consistent with the Purpose Council's stated sequencing.

**Dependencies/assets:**
- *Can reuse:* PowerPoint deck (poster-art background) as a visual foundation, likely needs extension for workshop-specific content; email sequence infrastructure for enrollment and reminders.
- *Still needed:* an actual workshop curriculum/run-of-show distinct from the webinar script (the VSL/"New Reveal" framework covers the webinar, not necessarily a 3–4 hour live session); a payment-processing and registration flow for a paid product (separate from the free webinar signup); a decision on live-group size/interaction model. These are real new build items — flagging them explicitly as the one place this offer suite asks for new work, which should be weighed against the Purpose Council's preference to strengthen existing delivery infrastructure before adding new fronts. Given this workshop is a direct execution of priority #1 (not a new front), it is more consistent with that directive than, say, standing up certification curriculum would be — but it is not zero-cost, and the curriculum gap should be resourced deliberately rather than assumed to already exist.

## Non-monetized element: Certification-school waitlist capture

Not an offer — a list-building action. At the workshop's close (not the webinar's), offer a no-cost waitlist opt-in for the future Online Purposeologist certification school (priority #2). This is intentionally **not** sold or priced here: per the Purpose Council review, the certification school is a scalable build front that should follow, not precede, proof that the books/seminars/workshops model converts and delivers. Capturing interest costs nothing new to build (reuses the email infrastructure) and gives priority #2 a warm list for when it is greenlit.
