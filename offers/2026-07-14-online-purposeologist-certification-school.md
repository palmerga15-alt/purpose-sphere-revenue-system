## Offer Name

**The Purposeologist Certification** — graduates are credentialed as a **Certified Purposeologist**.

(Naming note: "Online Purposeologist certification school" is the brief's internal shorthand for this priority; "Purposeologist Certification" is used here as the offer's working name, not as finished marketing copy — titles and copy are content-angle-strategist's job, not mine.)

## Summary

A structured, credentialing program that trains individuals to guide others through the Purposeology™ five-stage journey (find, focus, form, follow, fruition) and, at its higher tier, to deliver Purposeology content on DrG's behalf. This is the offer that converts Purposeology™ from a founder-delivered practice into a **teachable, licensable system** — the mechanism that lets the brand scale beyond DrG's own calendar. It is downstream of the books/workshops funnel (priority #1, already live via the July 15 webinar suite) and upstream of both global speaking (priority #3, certified graduates as an advocate/referral base) and Fortune 500 corporate training (priority #4, certified graduates as a licensed delivery arm for enterprise-scale engagements DrG cannot personally staff).

## Who It's For

Mapped to the brief's existing audience segments — no new segments introduced:

- **Individuals in transition** who completed the workshop (priority #1) and want to go deeper — the primary source pool, since they've already self-selected as ready to move past awareness (this is explicitly named in the webinar offer suite as the natural first cohort for this waitlist).
- **Psychology-adjacent practitioners** (coaches, counselors, clinicians, campus/wellness staff) — the research report's certification-pricing benchmarks (life-purpose coach certifications, AI-coaching certification) are drawn from this exact buyer type, suggesting it is a validated purchasing segment for a credentialing product even though the report doesn't test purpose/calling-specific demand within it (see Research Gaps below).
- **Faith-adjacent leaders** (pastors, ministry staff, chaplains) who want a credential to formalize purpose-guidance work they may already be doing informally — consistent with the brief's faith-adjacent segment and the report's finding of active pastor/practicing-Christian engagement with AI-and-meaning questions.
- **Veterans**, particularly those the "Operation: Rediscover Purpose" program and NuHorizons connection are already engaging — certification as a "translate your edge into a credential" path, consistent with the report's finding that veteran messaging performs better framed as opportunity than anxiety relief.
- **Corporate/L&D-adjacent individuals** (internal trainers, HR/L&D staff at target accounts) — a smaller, deliberate slice: seeding certified practitioners inside organizations is a plausible bridge to priority #4, though this is a forward-looking rationale, not a segment with its own demand evidence in hand (flagged in Research Gaps).

## Format / Duration

High-level program structure only — no internal 26-concept framework or Concept Dictionary content is described or implied below. The program is organized around the five public-facing stages already used across the brief (find, focus, form, follow, fruition), presented as the learning arc a Purposeologist is trained to guide others through, not as a syllabus of concepts.

- **Delivery mode:** Online, cohort-based with self-paced study components — a hybrid of the two benchmark models in the research report (fully self-paced life-coach certifications vs. the AI-coaching certification's structured monthly cohort/module format).
- **Length:** 6 months, mirroring the shorter end of the report's "3–9 month" benchmark range and matching the closest single named comparable (Life Purpose Coach Specialty Certification, ~6 months).
- **Structure (illustrative, not final curriculum):**
  - A foundational module set teaching the five-stage journey as a facilitation practice — how to help another person move through find → focus → form → follow → fruition — plus the Chronos/Kairos distinction and Purpose Fingerprint™ concept, both of which the brief already treats as external-facing.
  - Applied practicum: each candidate guides a small number of real "clients" (could be drawn from the workshop/webinar funnel) through part of the journey under supervision.
  - A capstone assessment and live oral review before certification is awarded.
- **Assessment/quality control:** Not yet designed — flagged under Dependencies below. A credentialing program without a real assessment bar risks devaluing the certification quickly; this needs deliberate design, not assumption.

## Deliverables and Outcomes

Framed around the five-stage journey, with no internal concept enumeration:

- Graduates earn the **Certified Purposeologist** credential — a recognized ability to guide another person through find, focus, form, follow, and fruition using Purposeology's method, without exposing them to (or requiring them to reconstruct) DrG's proprietary internal framework beyond what the program itself teaches them directly.
- Graduates receive a completed practicum record (real guided sessions, not simulated).
- Upper-tier (licensed) graduates receive defined rights to deliver a specific, bounded training format on Purposeology's behalf — the mechanism that makes priority #4 (Fortune 500 corporate training) scalable beyond DrG's personal bandwidth.
- All graduates become part of a practitioner community/directory — an asset that itself feeds priority #3 (global speaking) by giving DrG a visible, credentialed base of advocates and referral sources, and priority #5 (purposeology.ai) a pool of trained humans-in-the-loop if the platform needs credentialed practitioners for quality or escalation purposes down the line.

## Path to Adjacent Priorities

- **From priority #1 (books/seminars/workshops):** This offer's buyer pool is explicitly seeded by the existing funnel. The July 15 webinar offer suite already captures a free certification-school waitlist at the workshop's close (`/offers/2026-07-15-purposeology-ai-webinar-offer-suite.md`) — that list is this offer's warm launch audience and should be the first sales motion tested (see Minimal Proof Point below) before any broader marketing spend.
- **To priority #3 (global speaking):** Certified Purposeologists become a visible practitioner base — testimonials, referral sources, and local/regional ambassadors — that materially strengthens DrG's speaking pitch (a founder with a trained movement behind him, not just a book) in the Myles Munroe tradition the brief invokes.
- **To priority #4 (Fortune 500 corporate training):** This is the more structurally important link. A licensed delivery tier turns certified Purposeologists into the staffing/delivery arm that makes enterprise-scale corporate training economically viable — DrG cannot personally deliver at Fortune 500 scale, but a bench of licensed, quality-controlled practitioners can. This is the offer's clearest strategic reason for existing beyond direct tuition revenue.
- **To priority #5 (purposeology.ai):** Not a primary path, but worth noting: a defined, teachable five-stage facilitation method is also the clearest existing asset to eventually encode into an AI-guided product. This offer doing the work of externalizing the method (without exposing the internal 26 concepts) is preparatory groundwork either way.

## Dependencies / Assets Needed

**Already exists / reusable:**
- The five-stage journey framing (find, focus, form, follow, fruition) is already an established, externally-safe teaching structure per the brief.
- The webinar/workshop funnel (priority #1) already generates a warm waitlist for this offer at no additional build cost (per the existing offer suite).
- Email infrastructure for nurturing the waitlist is already built (per the offer suite).

**Still needed (real new-build items, not assumed to exist):**
- Full curriculum design and instructional content beyond the outward five-stage frame (module content, practicum design, facilitator guides).
- An assessment rubric and capstone review process credible enough to make the credential mean something.
- A delivery platform/LMS to run a cohort-based online program at scale.
- A legal/licensing framework for the upper tier specifically — defining what a "Corporate Delivery License" actually grants, royalty or fee structure, quality-control and brand-standard enforcement mechanisms (including making sure licensed deliverers uphold the brand standards: colors, "DrG Alfred Palmer" naming, geography framing, and the internal-IP boundary around the 26 concepts).
- Instructor/assessor capacity — someone (DrG himself initially, or a small trained team) has to actually run cohorts and assess capstones; this does not run on autopilot from day one.
- A practitioner directory/community infrastructure if the "visible base" benefit to priorities #3–4 is to be realized rather than just implied.

## Explicit Council Guidance Flag

**Opening this school is itself a new build front.** The Purpose Council's stated priority is strengthening proof and delivery infrastructure — i.e., proving the books/workshops model (priority #1) actually converts and delivers — **before** opening new fronts. A full certification school (curriculum, LMS, assessment, licensing legal work, instructor capacity) is unambiguously a new front by that standard, not an extension of existing delivery infrastructure the way the July 22 workshop is (see the existing offer suite's own note that the workshop, unlike a full certification build, is "a direct execution of priority #1... more consistent with that directive than, say, standing up certification curriculum would be").

**Recommended minimal proof point before a full launch:** a small **pilot cohort** — for example, 10–20 people drawn directly from the existing workshop/webinar waitlist, run manually (no LMS build, no formal licensing infrastructure, informal assessment by DrG personally) at little or no incremental build cost. This would validate:
1. Whether waitlisted individuals actually convert to paid certification interest at any real rate.
2. Whether the five-stage facilitation curriculum, taught at a basic level, produces graduates who can demonstrably guide someone else through the journey (the core promise of the whole priority).
3. What a defensible assessment bar even looks like, before formalizing one at scale.

A pilot cohort of this size is a proof point, not a launch — it lets the Council see real conversion and delivery data before committing to the LMS/legal/instructor-capacity build-out this offer would need at full scale. This recommendation is offered as an option for the Council's decision, not a default green light to build the full school.

## Research Gaps (explicitly flagged, not filled with assumption)

The research report (`/research/2026-07-purposeology-and-ai-webinar.md`) was built for the AI webinar, not this offer, and does not cover:
- **Direct demand signals for a purpose/calling-specific certification** — the report's benchmarks (life-purpose coach certifications, AI-coaching certification) are adjacent categories, not evidence that people specifically want to become certified in guiding *others* through a proprietary purpose-discovery method.
- **Direct competitor certification programs in the purpose/calling space specifically** — the report names competitors in the AI-and-purpose *content* space (Sinek, Ikigai 2.0) but none in the *certification/credentialing* space for purpose or calling coaching specifically.
- **Corporate appetite for a licensed-practitioner delivery model** — the report shows L&D buyers are currently sold skills/productivity almost exclusively and that a meaning/retention angle is open territory, but it does not test whether those buyers would accept training delivered by a licensed practitioner network versus DrG personally.

These should be routed back to market-signal-researcher as a follow-up brief if/when the Council decides to move beyond the pilot-cohort stage recommended above.
