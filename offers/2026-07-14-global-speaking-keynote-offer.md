## Offer Name

**DrG Alfred Palmer — Keynote & Speaking Engagements** (working name; titles/positioning language are content-angle-strategist's job, not architected here)

## Summary

A bookable keynote-speaking product: DrG delivers live (and, at lower fee, virtual) keynote addresses on purpose-driven leadership to faith/church-leadership, veteran-services, Black-leadership/HBCU, and general corporate-leadership audiences — the circuits the research confirms are active, dated, and reachable in 2026. This is not a new capability being built from scratch (DrG has a documented history of sharing platforms with Les Brown, Willie Jolley, George Fraser, and Myles Munroe), but it *is* the first time this capability is being packaged and marketed as a standing, bookable offer with its own pricing and asset infrastructure — see the Extending-vs-New-Front assessment below.

## Who It's For (Bookers, Not Attendees)

The buyer here is the **event organizer/booker**, not the individual attendee — a different buyer type than priorities #1–#2's direct-to-individual funnel. Mapped to the brief's audience segments plus one flagged addition:

- **Faith-adjacent leadership circuit** — church/ministry leadership conferences (e.g., Global Leadership Summit, Thrive Leadership). Maps directly to the brief's faith-adjacent segment.
- **Veteran-services circuit** — veteran-service-organization conferences (e.g., Warrior Symposium, NASWA Veterans Conference). Maps directly to the brief's veteran segment and DrG's U.S. Air Force background/NuHorizons connection.
- **Corporate/organizational buyers** — general keynote bookings at corporate leadership events. Maps directly to the brief's corporate/organizational segment and is this offer's clearest bridge to priority #4.
- **Black-leadership/HBCU circuit** (UNCF UNITE, George Fraser's Power Networking Conference) — **flagging this explicitly as not a segment named verbatim in the brief's audience list.** It is well-grounded in real, checkable relationships (the Titans of Public Speaking honor alongside Les Brown, Willie Jolley, and George Fraser; George Fraser's own conference), so treating it as a distinct addressable segment for the speaking stream specifically is a reasonable extension — but per the architecture rules, it needs the user's explicit sign-off as a formal segment rather than being silently folded into "corporate/organizational buyers."

Psychology-adjacent institutions (the brief's fifth segment) are not a natural fit for this specific offer and are not included here.

## Format / Duration

- **Core Keynote:** 45–60 minutes, live or virtual, plus Q&A. Standard single-session conference/corporate keynote format — matches the format the research's comparable speakers (Jolley, Shelton, Thomas) are booked for.
- **Keynote + Breakout/Workshop Add-On:** Core keynote plus a half-day breakout or workshop session for a smaller group (distinct from, but reusable-in-spirit with, the priority #1 workshop format) — for bookers wanting deeper engagement than a single mainstage slot.
- **Virtual delivery:** Lower-fee tier for remote keynote delivery, consistent with the research's finding that virtual fees for comparable speakers run roughly 40–60% of the live fee (e.g., Jolley $10K–$20K virtual vs. $15K–$50K live; Shelton $10K–$20K virtual vs. $50K–$100K live).
- This offer is **keynote/speaking only** — it is explicitly distinct from priority #4 (Fortune 500 corporate training, a multi-session enterprise engagement), though a speaking booking is this offer's primary path into that priority (see below).

## Extending Priority #1 vs. New Front — Explicit Assessment

This is a mixed case, and both readings are legitimate depending on which layer is being evaluated:

- **The underlying capability is an extension, not new-build.** DrG's ability to deliver a keynote, his published-book base, and his documented peer/platform history all already exist — none of this requires building new delivery infrastructure the way the certification school required a curriculum, LMS, and licensing framework from scratch. In that sense, pursuing speaking bookings now leans more toward "extending existing assets" than "opening a new front."
- **The go-to-market motion is genuinely new.** Packaging DrG's speaking as a standing, priced, marketed product — with a one-sheet, a video/reference asset base, a bureau strategy, and an active "successor to Myles Munroe" narrative — is not yet built and is not the current active GTM motion (the July 15 webinar is). Standing this up in parallel, before the webinar/workshop funnel (priority #1) has demonstrated conversion, is the kind of new-front expansion the Purpose Council's proof-before-new-fronts guidance is aimed at.
- **Recommended resolution:** treat this as **asset-reuse-first, opportunistic pursuit** for 2026 — chase the one genuinely time-sensitive, high-relationship-equity target (Fraser's conference) and the low-cost DMV-adjacent relationship-building (Warrior Symposium attendance) using only existing assets and near-zero incremental spend (the video capture piggybacks on an already-scheduled priority #1 event), **without** standing up the fuller go-to-market build (bureau signing, paid EPK production, aggressive up-tier pricing, active narrative marketing) until priority #1's funnel has produced real conversion data. This keeps near-term action inside the spirit of the Council's sequencing guidance while not forfeiting genuinely time-sensitive, low-cost opportunities that lead times would otherwise close off entirely.
