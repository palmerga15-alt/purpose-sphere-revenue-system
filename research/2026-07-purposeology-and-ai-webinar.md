# Market Signal Report: "Purposeology & AI" Webinar (July 15, 2026)

**Research question:** Do external market signals support the thesis "AI gives you information. Purposeology™ gives you the reason to use it," positioning Purposeology™ as the meaning-and-direction layer rather than a competitor to AI tools?

**Prepared by:** market-signal-researcher | **Date:** 2026-07-14 (webinar is T-1 day — see time-sensitive flags at end)

---

## 1. Demand Signals

There is strong, current, and rising public discussion at the exact intersection this webinar targets — AI capability anxiety plus a *meaning/purpose* framing (not just job-loss framing).

- **AI job-loss fear is high and rising.** Mercer's *Global Talent Trends 2026* survey (12,000 workers/leaders) found 40% of employees fear losing their job to AI, up from 28% two years prior. A Resume Now survey found 60% of U.S. workers expect AI to eliminate more jobs than it creates this year. An Express Employment/Harris Poll found 90% of U.S. job seekers worry about AI at work. Recent-graduate fear jumped from 64% (2025) to 89% (2026) on AI replacing entry-level roles. [Metaintro](https://www.metaintro.com/blog/40-percent-workers-fear-losing-job-to-ai-2026), [Monster](https://www.monster.com/career-advice/research/graduate-ai-readiness), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/carolinecastrillon/2026/06/26/why-ai-anxiety-is-making-workers-question-their-future/)
- **The discourse is explicitly moving from "job loss" to "purpose loss."** A March 2026 Psychology Today piece, *"It's Not Just Jobs That AI Is Taking—It's Our Purpose,"* argues: "We're building a world where being needed is optional. This isn't just economic disruption. It's existential and spiritual catastrophe," and proposes "Universal Basic Purpose, not just Universal Basic Income." [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/tech-happy-life/202506/its-not-just-jobs-that-ai-is-taking-its-our-purpose)
- **"Ikigai Risk" is an emerging named concept** in thought-leadership writing — "the potential loss of human purpose in an AI-augmented world" — showing the meaning-vs-AI framing has enough traction to get its own coined term. [Substack: Mike Kentz](https://mikekentz.substack.com/p/balancing-productivity-and-purpose), [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/human-flourishing-in-the-age-of-ai-b456b1de31c7)
- **Language people actually use:** "What's the point in learning anything?" / "What am I going to do with my life?" / "If AI can handle technical tasks, where do I fit?" (a 17-year-old questioning college major choice because "it won't exist when he graduates"). This existential-doubt framing shows up in both youth/student contexts and general workforce contexts. [Psychology Today via search summary], university crisis-readiness literature (Tandfonline, 2026)
- **Deskilling fear reads as purpose fear, not laziness fear:** 37% of job seekers worry AI will make employees "exert less effort" — read by researchers as fear of losing meaningful engagement, not a wish for less work.

**Read:** Demand for a "purpose layer over AI" message is well-timed. The market is actively re-framing AI anxiety from economic to existential terms — which is precisely the gap the webinar's thesis claims to fill.

## 2. Competitive Landscape

No direct competitor was found running a webinar/course with an identical framing ("AI gives information, X gives you the reason to use it") under a proprietary calling/purpose-discovery brand — but several adjacent players are active in this exact conceptual space, at higher-profile and higher-price tiers:

- **Simon Sinek — closest thought-leadership analog.** His May 2025/2026-recirculated Diary of a CEO episode, *"You're Being Lied To About AI's Real Purpose! We're Teaching Our Kids To Not Be Human!"* argues society fixates on AI's "destination" (output) and ignores the human "journey" (the process/struggle that builds identity) — a structurally similar distinction to "AI = information, Purposeology™ = the reason." Sinek's keynote fee is reported above $150,000, and he was a featured 2026 speaker at IAMPHENOM (HR/AI conference) and addressed SHRM26 on "purpose, trust and human connection." This is the highest-visibility competing voice on "AI vs. human purpose" and worth monitoring, not out-positioning head-on. [SingJuPost transcript](https://singjupost.com/simon-sinek-youre-being-lied-to-about-ais-real-purpose-doac-podcast-transcript/), [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/about/press-room/simon-sinek-challenges-shrm26-attendees-to-lead-with-purpose--tr), [Phenom](https://www.phenom.com/press-release/simon-sinek-start-with-why-iamphenom)
- **Book-format competitor:** *Ikigai 2.0: Meaning Beyond Work in the AI Age — A Guide to Human Flourishing When Machines Do the Jobs* (Shivakamat, Kindle Store) directly repackages the Japanese ikigai framework for the AI-anxiety moment — same audience insight, different (non-proprietary, non-faith-adjacent) framework. [Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.com/Ikigai-2-0-Meaning-Flourishing-Machines-ebook/dp/B0FLF2X64B) (price/full description not independently verified — fetch was blocked; flagged as unverified, do not cite pricing without re-checking)
- **AI-and-coaching webinars exist but are practitioner-facing, not consumer/purpose-facing** — e.g., Henley Business School's "AI and coaching: What does this mean for you as a human coach?" (Dr. Jonathan Passmore) targets coaches worried about AI replacing *them*, not end-clients seeking purpose. Positive Psychology Institute is running a 12-month "AI for Coaching" certification (July 2026–June 2027 cohort) priced at $149/month or $1,495/year lump sum, $995 bundled — a useful certification-pricing benchmark even though it's a different audience. [Delenta](https://www.delenta.com/blog/ai-coaching-trends-tools-2026), [Positive Psychology Institute]
- **General AI-and-career-reinvention courses** (Udemy: "Future Skills: Thriving in the AI Age," "Artificial Intelligence Ready Career Blueprint for 2026," "Succeed in the Age of AI") compete for the same anxious-worker attention but are skills/productivity framed, not meaning/calling framed — leaves the "reason to use it" positioning largely open at the mass-market course level.
- No evidence found of a competing offer using a faith-adjacent, veteran-specific, or "biblically-framed without denominational packaging" angle on AI + purpose — this appears to be white space specific to Purposeology's audience mix.

**Read:** The "AI = information, we = meaning" framing has real intellectual competition (Sinek, ikigai authors) but no direct competitor combining it with a proprietary discovery framework, faith-adjacent framing, and veteran/corporate dual-audience reach. Differentiation is available; message discipline relative to Sinek's higher-authority voice matters.

## 3. Audience Pain Points (by brief segment)

- **Individuals in transition:** Fear is shifting from "will I lose this job" to "will I lose my reason for getting up" — see Psychology Today "existential and spiritual catastrophe" framing and the "being needed is optional" language. Concrete self-talk found in research: "What's the point in learning anything?", "Where do I fit?"
- **Corporate/L&D buyers:** 2026 L&D reports (TalentLMS, Training Industry, Josh Bersin) show AI training investment is real ($400B+ market per Bersin) but overwhelmingly skills/productivity framed (72% cite efficiency, 70% cite future-skills prep) — almost none frame AI adoption in terms of employee meaning or purpose retention. This is a stated gap L&D buyers are not yet being sold against, consistent with Purposeology's "purpose-and-leadership" enterprise angle. [TalentLMS](https://www.talentlms.com/research/learning-development-report-2026), [Josh Bersin](https://joshbersin.com/2026/02/new-research-how-ai-transforms-400-billion-of-corporate-learning/)
- **Faith-adjacent / spiritual-but-not-religious:** Directly relevant 2026 data — 54% of practicing Christians now say they'd trust AI to help them find meaning/purpose in life, yet 57% see AI as a "looming threat," and 71% of pastors feel "cautious" about AI (40% "conflicted"). Lifeway's June 2026 "Brentwood Statement on AI and Christian Ministry" asserts "ask 'Should we?' before 'Can we?'" and "every human is an Imago Dei bearer; a machine is a mirror, not a person" — language that resonates with (but does not require replicating) Purposeology's red-letter/Jesus-centered-without-denominational-packaging approach. This is a live, actively-debated audience concern right now. [Christian Post](https://www.christianpost.com/news/most-pastors-practicing-christians-worry-about-ai-replacing-god.html), [NAE](https://www.nae.org/ai-church-role-kenny-jahng/)
- **Veterans:** Sentiment is more optimistic than anxious — multiple 2026 sources (Military.com, ClearanceJobs, Redeployable, Cloud Veterans) argue veterans are "uniquely positioned to thrive" because human-centered skills (leadership, adaptability, decision-making under pressure) resist automation, and new legislation (Improving Emerging Tech Opportunities for Veterans Act of 2026) is expanding AI-adjacent training pathways. Implication: veteran messaging may land better as "translate your edge into purpose-driven direction" rather than "AI anxiety relief" — the anxiety framing fits this segment less than others.
- **Psychology-adjacent institutions:** Campus counseling literature (2026) documents "future-oriented anxiety" and existential-crisis intervention models tied directly to AI displacement fears among students; a cited Journal of Clinical Psychology finding that people lacking meaning are three times more likely to have long-term depression reinforces the clinical relevance of a "purpose layer" pitch to this segment. [Tandfonline 2026](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/2331186X.2026.2695483)

Useful ceiling/floor markers for DrG's speaking-stream pricing conversations (not a recommendation — reporting only).

## 5. Timing/Channel Signals

- **Day/time:** 2026 webinar-benchmark data converges on Tuesday–Thursday, 10–11 AM or 1–2 PM (audience local time) as highest-attendance windows; Wednesday 11 AM Pacific/2 PM Eastern is cited as the single best slot, with ~81% live attendance. July 15, 2026 is a **Wednesday** — this happens to already align with the highest-performing benchmark day, a mild tailwind.
- **News environment going into July 15, 2026:** Active, AI-layoff-heavy news cycle — TechCrunch is running a running list of 2026 tech layoffs explicitly citing AI (Oracle, Amazon, Meta, Block, Google named), and HBR (July 2026) published research on how AI is raising, not lowering, the bar for what employers expect from knowledge workers. This raw anxiety is currently front-of-mind in the news cycle the webinar would launch into — a real-time tailwind for an anxiety-acknowledging, meaning-forward pitch, but also means audiences will be fatigued by AI-doom content; differentiation (direction/meaning vs. more doom-and-skills content) matters more than novelty of topic.

## Implications for the Pipeline

1. **Thesis is validated by current discourse**, not just plausible — the market has independently coined language ("Ikigai Risk," "Universal Basic Purpose") for exactly the gap Purposeology™ claims to fill. offer-architect can treat "AI gives information, we give the reason" as market-tested language, not a novel bet.
2. **Simon Sinek is the nearest high-authority voice in this exact conceptual lane.** content-angle-strategist should be aware of his "destination vs. journey" framing to differentiate rather than echo it — Purposeology's proprietary five-stage framework (find/focus/form/follow/fruition) and faith-adjacent, veteran, and corporate dual-audience reach are areas Sinek's content does not cover and are natural differentiators.
3. **Segment-specific angle strength varies:** faith-adjacent and psychology-adjacent segments show the most direct, current, quotable anxiety data to draw on; veteran messaging should lean toward "translate your edge" / opportunity framing rather than anxiety-relief framing, since veteran-focused coverage skews optimistic already; corporate/L&D buyers are currently being sold skills and productivity almost exclusively — a meaning/retention angle is largely unclaimed territory for offer-architect to design into a Fortune 500 pitch.
4. **Format/pricing reference points** (certification $3–8K typical, AI-coaching-certification comparable at ~$1,500/yr, keynote fees $2,500–$75,000+ depending on tier) are available as external benchmarks for offer-architect — these are reported as market context only, not as recommendations.
5. **Time-sensitive, act-before-July-15 items:**
   - July 15, 2026 is a Wednesday, which independently benchmarks as the best-performing webinar day/time slot — no schedule change needed, but if there is flexibility on time-of-day, 11 AM PT/2 PM ET is the highest-attendance window cited across multiple 2026 reports.
   - The news cycle right now is saturated with AI-layoff coverage (TechCrunch's running list, HBR's July 2026 piece) — content-angle-strategist may want to acknowledge this raw, current anxiety directly in opening hooks/subject lines rather than treating AI-job-fear as background context, since it is unusually front-of-mind this week.
